The pipeline

Reason in this order, every time

A wrong answer is usually a skipped stage, not a bad final choice. The order is the discipline.

Context→ See→ Read→ State→ Need→ Solve→ Predict→ Organize→ Reassess→ Adapt
Context
Score, clock, field position, personnel, what the last three possessions taught both sides. Context sets the price of a mistake.
See
Perception only. Collapsing See into Read is how you defend the picture you expected instead of the one in front of you.
Read
Height × Time × Balance, plus Orientation and Certainty.
State
Defensive, Neutral or Offensive — who is dictating. Scramble sits over the top.
Need
The rung you're actually on. The single strongest constraint on which actions are legal.
Solve
Weigh Risk, Consequence, Cost and Target, then choose one action.
Predict
Name what the opponent does in response — one beat, concretely. You can't cover a reaction you never named.
Organize
Move, Cover, Recover. Everyone has exactly one job. Recover is the one that gets dropped, and it decides whether the next rep starts in Scramble.
Reassess
Re-run See → Read. Mandatory. You acted, so this is a different situation now.
Adapt
Adjust the solve, or step the Need. Dropping a rung isn't failure; refusing to drop is.

State

Three states, and a layer over all of them

Defensive

They hold the initiative. You are responding.

Neutral

Neither side holds it. The next action decides who does.

Offensive

You hold the initiative. They are responding.

Scramble

A condition layer, not a fourth state — it overlays whichever state you're in, so always name both. Scramble means the map is stale: assignments are no longer where they were drawn. Reassess frequency goes up, and commitment thresholds go up with it.

Need

A ladder, climbed one rung at a time

Climbing is earned, not chosen. You move up when the rung below is actually satisfied — not when you'd prefer to be higher.

SurviveDon't lose it right now. Deny the immediate shot or the immediate turnover.
RecoverGet body, feet and position back to legal — repay the debt the last action created.
StabilizeRe-establish structure. Everyone knows who has whom again.
BuildAccumulate advantage. Shape, spacing, numbers.
PressureForce the opponent into a decision they don't want.
FinishConvert the advantage.

Rung-skipping is the most common error in the model. A player on Survive who reaches for a Build action doesn't get Build — they get a worse Survive. Name the rung before choosing the action.

Solve

Four weights on every candidate action

Risk
How likely is this to fail?
Consequence
If it fails, how bad? Independent of Risk — a high-risk, low-consequence action is often the right one.
Cost
What it spends even when it works: legs, position, Recovery Debt, a teammate's slot.
Target
What it's aimed at. Aim at the factor — "this takes Time." An action without a named target is effort, not a solve.

Vocabulary

Seven calls, used exactly

Never paraphrased, never blended, never swapped for a generic coaching synonym. The compression is the point: the call has to survive being shouted and mean the same thing to everyone who hears it.

Respect the X State Def / NeutralRung Stabilize +Layer Threat

The anchor point that generates the danger stays in your accounting even when the ball is elsewhere. Failing to respect it shows up one beat later as a Scramble that "came out of nowhere."

The Triangle speedup → counter → exit State OffensiveRung Build / PressureLayer Tempo

Three beats run as one unit. How you buy a missing factor instead of waiting for it. Illegal on Survive or Recover — the speedup beat borrows Balance you don't have.

Own space not lines State AnyRung AnyLayer Cost

Occupy and hold area. Don't chase a line or defend where someone just was. Space is cheap to hold and expensive to chase — the lowest-cost way to stay connected when structure is stale.

Go Stay Go State Def / NeutralRung Stabilize / PressureLayer Tempo

A tempo break inside a single approach. The Stay beat is information — it makes the opponent declare before you commit. Reversible by design, which is why it's the low-Certainty call.

Hip Pocket Over State DefensiveRung PressureLayer Initiative

Taking a factor away by position rather than by effort. A committing call: high Certainty only, and the debt has to be paid before it's legal.

Cover Cover Sit State DefensiveRung Survive → StabilizeLayer Recovery Debt

Two cover beats, then settle. Sit is the deliberate refusal to borrow — how you stop a Scramble compounding, not how you end a possession.

Oh Slide Go State Def / ScrambleRung Survive / StabilizeLayer Threat

Recognize, slide, commit. Fully committing: it creates Recovery Debt and hands someone else the Cover job, so it's only legal when Organize can actually fill that job.

Outer layers

Nine lenses, not nine stages

Reach for one when the pipeline gives a sound answer that keeps failing, or to explain why a correct solve was still the wrong solve. One lens, occasionally two — never a checklist of all nine.

Tempo

The rate at which decisions are demanded. A weapon independent of speed.

Initiative

Who is dictating. It can change hands without the ball moving.

Recovery Debt

Position and energy owed from a previous action, payable before the next one is legal. Usually why a rung can't be climbed.

Threat

What they could do, as distinct from what they're doing. Why you defend an empty space.

Pattern Memory

What this opponent has already shown tonight. It raises or lowers Certainty — and a good opponent will spend a rep buying a false one.

Error Classification

See, Read, State, Need, Solve, Organize and Execution errors are coached differently. Classify before correcting.

Cost

What an action spends when it succeeds. Sustainability is a Cost problem, not a Risk problem.

Pace

Your own rate of output. Pace is what you spend; Tempo is what you impose.

Pressure

Force applied to shrink their Time and Balance. Pressure that doesn't shrink a named factor is just effort.

The output

Four parts, and nothing else

1ReadH/T/B named concretely — "one step of cushion," not "limited."
2State + NeedBoth named. Scramble stated as a layer.
3ActionOne action, in the vocabulary. Not a menu, not a contingency tree.
4WhyOne line. If it needs a paragraph, the Read was wrong.

Worked example

Ball carrier picks his head up at the top — feet under him, stick up. You're a step and a half off, no help declared. He beat you from this exact look once already tonight.

  1. Read Height high, stick up and loaded. Time moderate, a step and a half of cushion and closing. Balance high, feet under him. Orientation square. Certainty low — he showed this look once already.
  2. State + Need Neutral. Need: Stabilize.
  3. Action Go Stay Go
  4. Why Low Certainty makes a commit illegal, so the Stay beat buys the information before I spend the cushion.
