# Vocabulary

These phrases are the instruction. **Use them exactly** — do not paraphrase,
translate to generic coaching language, or blend two into one call. Compression
is the point: the phrase has to survive being shouted, and it has to mean the
same thing to every player who hears it.

> The structural notes below describe **when each call is legal** — which Read,
> State, and Need rung it belongs to. Where a note reads as a mechanical gloss,
> defer to the LAB owner's definition; the call itself, and the situations it
> fits, are what this file is for.

---

## Respect the X

The anchor point that generates the danger. Respecting it means you do not let
it out of your accounting even when the ball is elsewhere — the threat is
positional and it persists.

- **Layer:** Threat, Pattern Memory
- **Legal from:** Defensive or Neutral, on any rung from Stabilize up
- **Use when:** attention is drifting toward the ball and away from the point
  that actually creates the shot. Failing to respect the X shows up one beat
  later as a Scramble that "came out of nowhere".

## The Triangle — speedup → counter → exit

A three-beat sequence, run as one unit. **Speed up** to force a reaction,
**counter** the reaction that comes, **exit** into the space it vacated.

- **Layer:** Tempo, Initiative
- **Legal from:** Offensive, on Build or Pressure
- **Use when:** you have Balance and need to manufacture Time or an angle. The
  Triangle is how you *buy* a missing factor rather than wait for it.
- **Do not call it on Survive or Recover** — the speedup beat borrows Balance
  you do not have, and it is Recovery Debt you cannot repay.

## Own space not lines

Occupy and hold area. Do not chase a line, trail a path, or defend where
someone just was.

- **Layer:** Cost, Threat
- **Legal from:** any state, especially under the Scramble layer
- **Use when:** a player is being pulled out of shape by movement. Space is
  cheap to hold and expensive to chase — this is the lowest-Cost way to stay
  connected when structure is stale.

## Go Stay Go

Approach, hold, re-approach. A tempo break inside a single approach.

- **Layer:** Tempo
- **Legal from:** Defensive or Neutral, on Stabilize or Pressure
- **Use when:** Certainty is low. The **Stay** beat is information — it makes the
  opponent declare before you commit. Reversible by design.

## Hip Pocket Over

Ride in the hip pocket, then take the over-top position.

- **Layer:** Initiative, Pressure
- **Legal from:** Defensive, on Pressure — requires the debt to be paid first
- **Use when:** Certainty is high and you are taking away a specific factor by
  position rather than by effort. This is a **committing** call; do not make it
  on a low-Certainty read.

## Cover Cover Sit

Two cover beats, then settle instead of committing.

- **Layer:** Cost, Recovery Debt
- **Legal from:** Defensive, on Survive, Recover, or Stabilize
- **Use when:** you cannot afford another commit, or the read is not clean
  enough to justify one. **Sit** is the deliberate refusal to borrow — it is how
  you stop a Scramble from compounding rather than how you end a possession.

## Oh Slide Go

Recognize, slide, commit.

- **Layer:** Threat, Initiative
- **Legal from:** Defensive or Defensive/Scramble, on Survive or Stabilize
- **Use when:** the danger is real and immediate and someone must leave their
  slot to meet it. Fully committing — it creates Recovery Debt and it hands
  someone else the **Cover** job, so it is only legal when Organize can actually
  fill that job.
