# Outer Layers

Nine reference lenses. They are **not pipeline stages** and must not be forced
into the pipeline order.

Reach for a layer when the pipeline produces a sound answer that keeps failing,
or when you need to explain *why* a correct solve was still the wrong solve.
One layer, occasionally two. Never a checklist of all nine.

---

**Tempo** — the rate at which decisions are being demanded. Tempo is a weapon
independent of speed: `Go Stay Go` breaks tempo without adding pace. If a player
is technically correct and still late, suspect Tempo before technique.

**Initiative** — who is dictating and who is responding. Initiative can transfer
without the ball moving. State names who has it; Initiative is the lens for how
it changes hands and what it costs to take.

**Recovery Debt** — position and energy owed from a previous action, payable
before the next one is legal. Every commit borrows. A player deep in debt has no
Balance to spend, which is why their Read looks fine and their execution does
not. Debt is the usual reason a Need rung cannot be climbed.

**Threat** — what the opponent could do, distinct from what they are doing.
Threat is why you defend an empty space. A threat you respect costs you nothing
if you are wrong about the timing and everything if you ignore it — this is the
lens behind `Respect the X`.

**Pattern Memory** — what this opponent, in this game, has already shown. It
raises or lowers Certainty. Two identical reads deserve different commitment
levels if one of them is the third repeat of a pattern. Pattern Memory is also
the trap: an opponent who knows you have it will spend a rep buying a false one.

**Error Classification** — errors are not interchangeable, and they are coached
differently:

| Error | What broke | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| See error | Didn't perceive it | Eyes, scan pattern, field width |
| Read error | Perceived, misjudged H/T/B | Reps against that specific look |
| State error | Misjudged who holds initiative | Slow the state call |
| Need error | Right read, wrong rung — usually rung-skipping | Name the rung out loud |
| Solve error | Right rung, wrong action | Vocabulary drilling |
| Organize error | Right action, jobs collided or Recover unfilled | Communication |
| Execution error | Everything right, body failed | Physical, not decisional |

The costly confusion is coaching an Execution error as a Read error, or a Need
error as a Solve error. Classify before correcting.

**Cost** — what an action spends when it *succeeds*. Distinct from Risk, which is
about failing. Sustainable defense is a Cost-management problem: a solve that
works every time but costs a full-field recovery each rep is not repeatable.

**Pace** — the athlete's own rate of movement and output. Pace is what you spend;
Tempo is what you impose. Confusing them produces players who run harder to fix
a timing problem, which makes both worse.

**Pressure** — force applied to shrink the opponent's Time and Balance. Pressure
is also a Need rung; the layer is the wider question of how much is being applied
across the whole field, by whom, and at what Cost. Pressure that does not shrink
a named factor is just effort.
