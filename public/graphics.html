How to draw on the court

Zones use the LAB grid. O = opponent side, Y = your side, then the zone number 1–9.

P1 Y1            your player (blue chip)
D1 O3            opponent (red chip)
ball Y1 > O7 1   ball path, step "1" at the end
ball Y2 ~> O1    arced ball (drops, lobs)
move Y1 > Y5 2   player movement (yellow)
zone O7 O8       highlight zones
hot O5           highlight in red
chip X Y5 orange any label, any colour
note Y5 Hold     small text label
// comment

O7-8 = seam between 7 and 8 · Y7^ = front of the zone (at the net) · Y1_ = back of the zone (baseline) · 0.5,0.9 = exact spot (across, down).
